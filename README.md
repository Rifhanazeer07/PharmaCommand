# PharmaCommand – Pharmaceutical Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

PharmaCommand is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze pharmaceutical sales performance across products, sales channels, sales teams, and geographical regions. It presents key performance indicators and visual insights to support business performance analysis.

## Dataset Details

**Dataset:** Pharmaceutical Company Wholesale-Retail Data

**Source:** [Kaggle – Pharmaceutical Company Wholesale-Retail Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/krishangupta33/pharmaceutical-company-wholesale-retail-data/data)

- **Time Period:** 2017–2020
- **Countries Analyzed:** Germany and Poland
- **Analysis Areas:** Products, customers, distributors, sales channels, quantities, prices, and sales teams

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

Data preparation was performed using Power Query before developing the dashboard.

- Identified and removed duplicate records.
- Corrected negative Quantity values.
- Checked data quality and validated the prepared data.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence and Analytics

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, and Total Products
- Sales evolution across years
- Sales by channel, product, and country

### 2. Sales and Channel Analysis
- Average Order Value, Average Price, Average Quantity, and Total Quantity
- Sales team performance
- Channel and sub-channel performance
- Monthly sales trends and yearly sales distribution

### 3. Product and Regional Analysis
- Total Products and Total Customers
- Total Quantity and Average Order Value
- Product class performance and product class mix
- Country performance across Germany and Poland
- Top cities by sales
- Average price by product

The dashboard includes interactive filters for channel, sub-channel, and year.
